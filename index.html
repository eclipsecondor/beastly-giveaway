About the Beastly Giveaway

The 2026 Beastly Giveaway is a global opportunity designed to reward dedication, community, and belief — on a scale that has never been attempted before.

From millions of applicants worldwide, 50 participants will be randomly selected to share a monumental $5,000,000 USD prize pool — ensuring every selected individual walks away with $100,000.

But this is only the beginning. The selected 50 advance into a second phase, where community voting determines the Top 10 finalists — transforming the giveaway into a shared global experience.

These finalists will join MrBeast on a luxury, multi-destination tour, gaining behind-the-scenes access, global exposure, and opportunities typically reserved for inner-circle collaborators.

The finalist with the highest number of votes will be crowned the #1 Winner and awarded a Lamborghini supercar — a moment that defines what the Beastly Giveaway stands for: scale, generosity, and unforgettable impact.

How the Beastly Giveaway Works

01

Apply Online

Participants from anywhere in the world can apply by completing the official Beastly Giveaway application form. The process is free and takes only a few minutes.

02

Eligibility Check

All applications are verified to ensure participants meet the basic requirements, including being 18 years or older and following MrBeast across his official social platforms.

03

Random Selection

From all eligible entries, 50 participants are randomly selected. Each selected participant automatically receives $100,000 as part of the $5,000,000 prize pool.

04

Voting Stage

The 50 selected participants advance to the voting phase. Each participant receives a unique voting link that can be shared with friends, family, and supporters. Participants are also allowed to vote for themselves.

05

Top 10 & Final Winner

The Top 10 participants with the highest number of votes win an exclusive luxury trip with MrBeast to three selected destinations. The participant with the most votes overall is crowned the #1 Winner and receives a Lamborghini supercar.

Operations & Strategy

Trust, Transparency & Communication

The Beastly Giveaway is built on transparency, fairness, and clear communication at every stage of the process.

Clear Selection Process

All participants who meet the eligibility requirements are entered into a random selection process. From these entries, 50 participants are selected fairly and without bias.

Direct Communication Only

Selected participants are contacted only via the email address provided during application. All instructions for the next stage, including voting access, are delivered directly and clearly.

Community-Driven Voting

During the voting stage, participants may share their unique voting link with friends and supporters worldwide. Participants are also allowed to vote for themselves, ensuring an open and competitive process.

Respect for Participant Data

Personal information submitted during the application process is used solely for giveaway administration and participant communication. Data is handled responsibly and never shared publicly.
